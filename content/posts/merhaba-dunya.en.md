For a long time I wanted a single place to collect what I write and what I build. Social accounts get scattered, platforms shut down, and algorithms decide what gets seen. Your own domain and your own site belong to you.

## What will be here?

I plan to write under three main themes:

- **Game development** — projects I am working on, problems I run into, and how I solve them
- **Technology and networking** — system design, infrastructure, and things I learn along the way
- **Finance** — markets, data analysis, and where they intersect with technology

These may look unrelated, but to me they are different faces of the same curiosity: understanding how a system actually works.

## How this site works

This site is deliberately simple. No build step, no framework, no database:

```text
static HTML + CSS + JavaScript
        ↓
    GitHub Pages
        ↓
  emrebiltekin.me
```

Posts live in the repository as Markdown, and the site renders them in the browser. Which means:

| Property | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly cost | $0 |
| Server maintenance | None |
| Who owns the content | Me |

> The nicest thing about keeping it simple is that it still works when you come back two years later.
